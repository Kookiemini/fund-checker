Two kinds of annual return, and why. Geometric (CAGR) is the compounded yearly growth of money: the right number to compare with an ad, so the gap uses it. Arithmetic is the average daily return times 252: Sharpe ratio, CAPM and alpha are built on average returns, so they use it. For a volatile fund the arithmetic number is higher than the geometric one (volatility drag), which is why both are shown and labelled.
