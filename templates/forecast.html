How to read this

  • Middle scenario: half of the replayed futures ended higher, half lower.
  • Bad scenario: only 1 future in 20 ended lower. Good scenario: only 1 in 20 ended higher. Real life can still be outside this range.
  • The longer you hold, the wider the range in dollars, but the smaller the chance of ending below what you put in.
  • All numbers are in US dollars of the future, before inflation and taxes.
