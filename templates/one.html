  • Forecast: {{ "{:,}".format(forecast.PATHS) }} futures are rebuilt from blocks of {{ forecast.BLOCK }} real months in a row (circular block bootstrap, seed {{ forecast.SEED }}). The chance to get your % is the share of futures that ended at least where steady growth at your % (with the same top-ups) would end.
  • How much to add: for each of the {{ "{:,}".format(forecast.PATHS) }} futures the site finds the exact monthly top-up that reaches the goal; "50% chance" is the middle of these numbers, "90% chance" is enough in 9 futures out of 10.

  • Rule Lab: monthly returns of SPY and cash at the 3-month Treasury rate; the position in a month is the signal of the month before, so the rule never uses future prices.
